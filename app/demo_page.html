ElevenLabs Agents demo

Eliza answers every lead, instantly, in Spanish.

A voice agent for a commercial real estate business in Toluca and Metepec, Mexico. She qualifies the inquiry in a natural conversation, then a backend scores the lead and saves it to a Notion pipeline for same-day follow-up.

Try it: click the call button in the bottom-right corner.

Eliza speaks Spanish. You could say: "Hola, busco un local en renta en Metepec para una cafetería, de unos 100 metros."

Calls are limited to 4 minutes. Please use made-up names and numbers: what you say becomes a lead in a demo database.

How it works

  1. Conversation (ElevenLabs). Eliza asks one question at a time, offers budget ranges when the caller is unsure, and reads the details back for confirmation.
  2. Server tool. After the caller confirms, she calls save_lead, an authenticated webhook on a FastAPI backend. Conversation ID and caller ID come from dynamic variables, not from the model.
  3. Deterministic logic. The backend validates the data, scores the lead and computes the callback time from business hours, then writes the lead to Notion.
  4. Close. The backend's reply tells Eliza exactly when a person will call back, so she never promises a time the team can't keep.
